Is It Down?

Is that site down, or is it just you?

Enter any website address. We check it from Cloudflare's network and tell you what came back.

One-time setup: paste your Worker URL (the one wrangler deploy printed). It's saved in this browser, or you can hard-code it as DEFAULT_API in this file.

Watchlist

    Sites you add show up here with their recent response times.
    It says "up" but I can't open it. Why?
    • This tool checks from Cloudflare's servers, not from your phone or ISP. A site can be fine globally and still be blocked or throttled on your network.
    • Try a different network (mobile data vs. Wi-Fi), a VPN, or public DNS such as 1.1.1.1 or 8.8.8.8.
    • "Up, but refused our check" (HTTP 401/403/429) means the server is running but rejects automated requests.
    • If it says the domain doesn't resolve, the name is mistyped, expired, or its DNS is broken.
